WirelessLab@UCLA · 2024–

Beam refinement

Full-duplex base stations must pick a beam pair while self-interference and cross-link interference keep shifting. Probing every pair is too expensive, and prior methods like STEER+ spend airtime on local searches that repeat as users move. We model self-interference across all candidate pairs with a transformer neural process, probe the pair with the highest plausible performance, and stop once the best pair so far is likely near-optimal. On measured 28 GHz data, it probes about 10 of 625 candidate pairs on average and outperforms STEER+ and Bayesian optimization at the same airtime.

RemoteRF

Run your own code, on your own machine, against a real radio across campus, as if it were plugged into your laptop. Built from a few radios on a USB hub into infrastructure that has run continuously for two years and is now part of every offering of UCLA's undergraduate and graduate communications courses.
